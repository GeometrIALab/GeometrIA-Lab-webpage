GeometrIA, the AI lab of the Brazilian Center for Geometry (CBG), invites the Brazilian mathematical community to take part in the survey “AI and the practice of mathematics”, on experiences, opinions and priorities related to artificial intelligence in research, teaching and academic life.

## Who can take part

The survey is addressed to people whose academic, scientific, educational or professional activities relate to Mathematics or Applied Mathematics, including students, lecturers, researchers and other professionals. It covers Brazilians living in Brazil or abroad, and foreigners with academic, scientific or professional ties to mathematics in Brazil.

We want to hear from you, whether or not you use AI tools. The experiences of those who do not use these tools, or who have chosen to avoid them, are essential to the survey.

## What the questionnaire covers

The questionnaire covers practices of use, authorship and credit, verification of results, teaching and assessment, supervision, confidentiality and conditions of access. It also asks about priorities for action by institutions, scientific societies and funding agencies, welcoming diverse perspectives.

## How to take part

The survey is open until **15 November 2026**. Answer in the language you prefer.

- [Answer in English](https://forms.gle/4HaLqnsGmHPxUxmm7)
- <span lang="pt-BR">[Responder em português](https://forms.gle/J88USKUodNS3UrBx6)</span>
- <span lang="es">[Responder en español](https://forms.gle/sPTkoZnDuqNTFR5AA)</span>

Completing the whole questionnaire takes about 30 minutes, but you can answer only the questions you wish and leave the rest blank. Participation is voluntary. The aggregated results will be made public in Portuguese and English, and individual answers will remain confidential.

## Help spread the word

We ask scientific societies, graduate programme coordinators, departments and colleagues to share this invitation with their networks, including people who do not usually follow discussions about AI.

Thank you for taking part and for helping to spread the word.

## Who is running the survey

The survey is run by the GeometrIA team (CBG, IMECC/UNICAMP), Henrique Sá Earp, Tomás S. R. Silva and Edward Hirst, with co-authors Daniel Fadel (ICMC/USP) and Cristiano Torezzan (FCA/UNICAMP).

Questions and comments can be sent to [geometria.lab@cbg.unicamp.br](mailto:geometria.lab@cbg.unicamp.br).
